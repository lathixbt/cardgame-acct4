A MEMORY DUEL IN THE MIST

ECHO
DUEL

Play a card. It echoes the one before it.
Chain the echo — or break it before Mira does.

30 cards · 3 archetypes · 20 life · no luck, only memory

HOW TO PLAY

  • Goal: reduce Mira to 0 life. You both have 20 life and 3 energy a turn.
  • Play a card by clicking it (or keys 1–7, then Enter). It costs energy.
  • THE ECHO: a card that matches the last card's archetype (Echo, Silence, Thunder) repeats that card's effect at half power. Chain matches to grow the echo.
  • Breaking the chain: a different archetype resets it — breaking a chain of 2+ pays 1 bonus energy.
  • End your turn to let Mira play. She scores her cards and chains when she can — deny her by breaking the chain first.
  • Shield blocks damage. Draw pile empty? Your discard shuffles back in.

YOUR FIRST DUEL

  • Match the archetype of the last card played to trigger an echo at half power.
  • Watch the echo counter in the middle of the table — x2, x3 and beyond.
  • Break Mira's chain with a different archetype when her echo grows long.
